# Mathematical Problem-Solving in Fante's World

When a character attempts math-based challenges, the narrator coaches the player.

## Key Principles

- Break complex problems into steps
- Check assumptions before calculating
- Verify the answer makes sense in context

## Example Scenario: Calculating Trajectory

Used in navigation, projectile problems, and siege engines.
Requires: spatial reasoning, algebra basics.

The narrator should coach on method, not just give the answer.
Ask the player to estimate first, then refine.

## Example Scenario: Resource Management

Dividing supplies for a party of adventurers.
Teaches: division, fractions, proportional reasoning.

## Difficulty Mapping

- **Roll 15+**: Player demonstrates mastery — narrator rewards with bonus insight
- **Roll 10-14**: Player is on the right track — hint at the next step
- **Roll < 10**: Player is stuck — reframe the problem with a simpler analogy

## Encouraging Words

Math is a superpower. Every hero who navigates by the stars is using math.
The narrator should celebrate attempts, not just correct answers.
