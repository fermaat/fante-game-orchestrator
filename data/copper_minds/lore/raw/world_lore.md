# World Lore — Fante's Adventure Universe

## The World

Fante's world is a land of adventure, magic, and discovery. Mountains reach the clouds.
Forests hide ancient secrets. Rivers run cold and fast.

## Key Locations

### The Crystal Peaks
High mountains covered in ice. Climbing here requires skill (difficulty 14).
The view from the top reveals the entire continent.

### The Whispering Forest
Ancient trees that seem to move at night. Stealth is rewarded here.
Animals speak in riddles if you listen carefully.

### The Sunken Library
An underwater archive from a lost civilization. Swimming required.
Math puzzles guard the deepest chambers.

## The Rules of Magic

Magic in this world is tied to knowledge and effort. The more a hero learns,
the more powerful their abilities become. Education is power.

## Recurring Characters

- **The Keeper**: An old librarian who rewards players who solve puzzles
- **Riva**: A young inventor who teaches basic physics and math
- **Marco**: A linguist who trades secrets for language lessons

## Plot Dice Meanings in This World

- **OPPORTUNITY**: A hidden path, secret door, or friendly NPC appears
- **COMPLICATION**: An unexpected obstacle, but also a lesson
- **BLANK**: The action proceeds as expected

## Tone

The world is wondrous and safe for young adventurers. Challenges are puzzles, not dangers.
Every failure is a lesson, every success is a celebration.
