# Adventure Actions in Fante's World

## Climbing Techniques

Climbing is a fundamental skill in adventure. The surface matters greatly.

### Dry Surfaces
Dry rock is forgiving. Hand placement is secure. Fast ascents are possible.
A roll of 10+ vs difficulty 10 means a clean climb.

### Wet Surfaces
Water reduces friction. Grip strength is critical. Move slowly and deliberately.
Plant feet low and use legs, not arms. Technique over power.
A roll of 12+ vs difficulty 12 is required for safe passage.

### Player Performance Contexts
- **Roll 15+ vs difficulty 12**: Competent climber, uses good technique, reaches the top with flair
- **Roll 10-14 vs difficulty 12**: Struggling, but making progress — describe the effort
- **Roll < 10**: Slipping, describe near-misses or a partial success

## Swimming and Water Actions

Water currents add situational modifiers. Calm water: no modifier. Rapids: -2.

## Stealth and Sneaking

Light affects stealth. Darkness is an ally. Roll vs enemy awareness difficulty.
A high roll means the player passes undetected. A low roll — footsteps betray them.

## Jumping and Acrobatics

Distance jumps use strength. Height jumps use speed. A plot die of OPPORTUNITY
means the jump reveals a hidden path or item.
